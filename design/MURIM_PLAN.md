# Murim World — Full Life-Sim Plan (draft v1)

Goal: one world (ยุทธภพ) that is fun to play for hours, feels like *living* a life in the jianghu —
BitLife's year-by-year loop + Taiwu's persistent generations + manhwa drama + Three Kingdoms war era.
No "[ระบบ]", no regression, no cross-world. Every other world is locked until murim is 100%.

---

## 1. Design pillars (what we borrow from where)

| Source | What we take |
|---|---|
| **BitLife** | Tap +1 year, short random events with 2–4 choices, menus for everything (activities, relationships, jobs, crime), ribbons at death, play on as your child. Small actions with consequences that pile up. |
| **The Scroll of Taiwu** | Generations: the world keeps going after you die. Feuds, sect reputations, your techniques and your village carry forward. Sworn brotherhood, blood feuds, marriage, raising heirs. |
| **Murim manhwa** | Humiliated-genius → rise. Arrogant young masters. Hidden masters (old beggar, sweeper monk). Fortuitous encounters (기연). Qi deviation (주화입마). Crippled dantian. Orthodox vs unorthodox vs Demonic Cult. Young Dragon rankings, nicknames (별호). |
| **Wuxia dramas (Jin Yong style)** | Jianghu codes vs imperial law. Master–disciple bonds, succession fights, betrayal and redemption. Escort agencies, Beggar Sect, heroes' gatherings, lost manuals, poison clans, love across factions. |
| **Three Kingdoms (สามก๊ก)** | A war era inside the timeline: rebellion → warlords → three states. Serve a lord, sworn brothers, strategist path (smarts), famous battles, loyalty vs ambition. |
| **Wandering Sword / RotK XIII** | NPC affinity with thresholds (gift / talk / spar). Bond levels (acquaintance → friend → close → sworn/married). Gifts work better on the right mood/preference. |

Fun rules we follow:
1. **Every year should offer a decision** (at least one event or a tempting menu option).
2. **Slow, visible growth** — you feel your internal energy go from "3 years" to "60 years".
3. **Risk you choose** — pushing cultivation, fighting, crime, war all can kill you.
4. **Your past matters** — grudges, favors, children, sect history come back later (also across generations).
5. **Realistic odds** — most people stay 3rd/2nd-rate. Reaching the top is rare and memorable.

## 2. Setting & time

- **The Central Plains (จงหยวน)** with regions: Northern Plains, Jiangnan (rich water towns), Sichuan (Tang clan, Emei, Qingcheng),
  Western frontier (Kunlun, Demonic Cult mountains), Southern wilds (poison, Yunnan), Capital.
- **One continuous timeline** (year counter, e.g. "ปีที่ 312 แห่งราชวงศ์...").
  World eras rotate: **Peace** → **Corruption/Famine** → **Chaos (warlords / Three Kingdoms era)** → **Unification** → Peace…
  Murim events on top: **Orthodox–Demonic War** (~every 60–100 yrs), plagues, Heroes' Gatherings, Demonic Cult resurgence.
- Money: **อีแปะ/เหวิน (copper)**, **ตำลึงเงิน**, **ตำลึงทอง**. Prices change with era (famine = rice ×5).

## 3. Birth

Pick or roll (BitLife style):
- **Family class**: peasant · fisher/hunter · artisan · merchant · scholar-gentry · minor official · martial family (one of the Five Great Families or a small clan) · sect orphan · beggar · (rare) noble.
- **Region** (affects which sects recruit you, food, dangers).
- **Born year** → which era you live through.
- **Innate traits** (replace "talents" window): meridian quality / bone structure (근골), constitution
  (e.g. *Nine Yin Meridians* — gifted but dies young unless cured; *Heaven-born Divine Strength*), personality, looks.
- Family members get personalities (strict father, gambling uncle, sickly mother…) — they drive events.

## 4. Stats

- Base (BitLife): **สุขภาพ · ความสุข · สติปัญญา · หน้าตา**.
- Murim:
  - **ลมปราณ (internal energy)** shown as *years of cultivation* — "กำลังภายใน 12 ปี". Classic and very readable.
  - **ร่างกาย (external)** — strength/stamina, decays with age.
  - **ความเข้าใจ (comprehension)** — speed of learning techniques, chance to create your own.
  - **ชื่อเสียง (fame)** and **ธรรมะ–มาร (alignment)** (already exists), **บุญคุณ–ความแค้น ledger** (exists).
- **Realms** (Korean murim ladder, rare at the top):
  ยอดฝีมือขั้นสาม (삼류) → ขั้นสอง (이류) → ขั้นหนึ่ง (일류) → จุดสูงสุด (절정) → เหนือจุดสูงสุด (초절정)
  → ขั้นหลอมรวม (화경) → ขั้นลึกล้ำ (현경) → เหนือความเป็นความตาย (생사경).
  Rough targets for a whole population: 60% never martial, 25% 3rd-rate, 10% 2nd, 4% 1st, <1% peak+, ~1 per generation at 화경+.
- Realm raises lifespan (peak +10, 화경 +30, 현경 +60) and slows aging.

## 5. Life stages (what you can do each age)

| Age | Life | Typical choices |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 | Baby (no actions) | Only events happen to you. |
| 3–6 | Child | Play, chores, follow parent to work, first lessons, village fights, find stray animal. |
| 7–12 | Youth | Village school (learn characters), apprenticeship, herding, **sect entrance tests (8–12)**, meet a hidden master, bullies. |
| 13–17 | Teen | Sect disciple life (outer → inner → core), chores & sect rules, sect tournaments, first love, trade apprentice, exam study, run away to the jianghu. Coming-of-age ceremony. |
| 18–30 | Young adult | Descend the mountain, wander, jobs, escort missions, marriage via matchmaker, sworn brothers, young-dragon ranking, war conscription. |
| 31–50 | Prime | Master, take disciples, found sect/clan/business, sect politics, alliance wars, serve a warlord, revenge arcs. |
| 51+ | Elder | Closed-door retreat, pass on techniques, write a manual, pick successor, family disputes over inheritance, death & funeral. |

## 6. Careers (job ladders with yearly salary & events)

- **Common**: farmer, fisher, woodcutter, hunter, porter, waiter → teahouse/inn owner, storyteller, gambler.
- **Craft**: blacksmith (forge weapons — your sword can become famous), tailor, carpenter.
- **Medicine**: herbalist → physician → divine physician (cure injuries/poison for others, Valley-of-Medicine style).
- **Trade**: peddler → merchant → caravan master → merchant guild.
- **Escort agency (สำนักคุ้มภัย)**: escort contracts with route + bandit risk, rank up to agency head.
- **Government**: constable (มือปราบ) → chief constable; soldier → officer → general (war era); **scholar path**: county → provincial → palace exams; in Three Kingdoms era → serve a lord as **strategist (เสนาธิการ)**.
- **Sect path**: outer disciple → inner → core → elder → sect leader.
- **Religious**: Shaolin monk / Wudang Taoist (celibacy rules!), Beggar Sect ranks (pouches).
- **Dark**: bandit, pirate, thief, **assassin organization (살수)**, poisoner, Demonic Cult member, Black Way alliance.

## 7. Yearly activities menu (BitLife-style)

**ฝึกฝน (Training)**
- Cultivate internal energy (+years; pushing hard = qi deviation risk 주화입마).
- Train body, practice a technique (mastery up), meditate (reduce deviation, +happiness).
- **Closed-door retreat** (1–3 years skip, big gains, can fail).
- Spar with peers, ask master for guidance, read a manual, **wander for a fortuitous encounter** (cliff fall → hermit, cave manual, thousand-year ginseng, spirit beast).

**ยุทธภพ (Jianghu)**
- Teahouse gossip → rumors become quests (treasure maps, wanted villains, beauty contests, tournaments).
- Challenge someone (spar or life-and-death duel), take bounties, escort jobs.
- Attend **Martial Tournament / Heroes' Gathering**, visit another sect, Murim Alliance duties, hunt Demonic Cult.

**ชีวิต (Life)**
- Work, rest, doctor/herbalist, temple prayer/fortune telling, medicine market (pills), gambling den, wine house, buy weapon/horse/clothes, travel between regions.
- **Festivals by season**: New Year, Lantern Festival, Qingming (sweep ancestors' graves), Dragon Boat, Qixi (lovers), Mid-Autumn — each with its own events.

**ครอบครัว & ความสัมพันธ์**
- Visit/support parents (filial piety → fame), **3-year mourning** when a parent dies (no marriage/office, + fame).
- Matchmaker, betrothal gifts, wedding, children's education, choose heir, disown.
- **Master–disciple**: kneel ceremony, master can expel you; avenge your master.
- **Sworn brotherhood** (peach garden oath) — strongest bond, betrayal is a huge event.
- Affinity 0–100 with thresholds (talk/gift/spar/help); special story unlocks at 60 / 80 / 100.

**อาชญากรรม (Crime)**
- Steal, rob travelers, poison, assassinate, frame someone → wanted poster, bounty hunters, jail, exile.

## 8. Core systems

### 8.1 Martial techniques (collectible, the heart of murim)
- Types: sword · saber · fist · palm · finger · spear · staff · qinggong · hidden weapons · poison · medicine · **internal method**.
- Grades: common → sect basic → sect secret → legendary (lost arts).
- Mastery: เริ่มเรียน → ชำนาญน้อย → ชำนาญใหญ่ → สมบูรณ์ (+ "เหนือรูปแบบ" at 화경).
- Sources: sect, master, found manual, stolen manual (sect hunts you), traded, **create your own** (high comprehension, late life).
- Incompatibility: orthodox + demonic internal methods together = deviation risk (manhwa trope).

### 8.2 Combat & injury
- Duel power = realm + internal years + technique mastery + weapon + health + age modifier + luck.
- Outcomes: win · draw · internal injury (years to heal) · **crippled dantian** (lose all martial power; rare cure) · lost limb · death.
- Spar (non-lethal) vs life-and-death duel (choose before).
- Poison & antidotes (Tang clan, southern poison sects). Illness, epidemics, aging decline after 50.

### 8.3 Factions (persist across generations)
- **Orthodox**: Nine Sects One Gang (Shaolin, Wudang, Emei, Kunlun, Huashan, Kongtong, Zhongnan, Diancang, Qingcheng + Beggar Sect)
  and Five Great Families (Namgung, Moyong, Jegal, Hwangbo, Sama / Sichuan Tang, Peng as variants) → **Murim Alliance**.
- **Unorthodox (Black Way)**: bandit lords, killer organizations, gambling/brothel gangs → Black Way Alliance.
- **Demonic Cult**: Heavenly Demon, demonic arts, internal politics.
- Each faction: stance toward you, rules, signature techniques, strength that rises/falls over decades. Wars between them.

### 8.4 Your own sect / clan / business
- Found a sect or martial family: buildings, disciples, territory, side businesses (escort agency, inn, medicine hall), reputation, rival sects.
- Sect survives after you die and appears in later lives.

### 8.5 Rankings & nicknames
- **Ten Greatest under Heaven**, **Young Dragon list** (under 30), regional lists. NPCs move in/out yearly.
- **Nickname (ฉายา)** given by the jianghu from deeds — "ดาบโลหิตแห่งเสฉวน", "หมัดเมตตา" — shown with your name.

### 8.6 Era engine (incl. Three Kingdoms era)
- Peace/chaos cycles change jobs, prices, danger, events.
- Chaos era: Yellow-Turban-like rebellion → warlords → three states. Join a lord, fight battles (big war events with strategy choices),
  become a famous general or strategist, or protect your village. Option: real Three Kingdoms heroes as NPCs (see open questions).

## 9. Generations & Chronicle (replaces "system/rebirth")

On death:
1. **Play as your child** (inherit money, weapon heirloom, family feuds, sect position).
2. **Play as your chosen disciple** (inherits taught techniques — Taiwu-like successor).
3. **New person in the same world, 1–100 years later.**

The world remembers (**บันทึกยุทธภพ** tab):
- Timeline of every past life + major world events.
- Your sect/business still exists (stronger or fallen). Your manual can be found in a ruin. Your tomb can be visited (or robbed).
- Descendants appear as NPCs; enemies' heirs come for revenge; favors can be repaid to your grandchildren.
- Teahouse storytellers tell your legend; ranking lists show "legends of past generations".
- Removed: regression, cross-world, soul shop. Achievements/ribbons stay (BitLife ribbons at death).

## 12. Decisions (answered 2026-10-19)

1. **All characters fictional.** Three Kingdoms era uses invented warlords/heroes/strategists with their own names.
2. **Multiple wives/concubines: yes, realistic.** Main wife vs secondary wives, household rank, jealousy, favoritism, children's
   inheritance fights, wives' families' politics.
3. **Dark: manhwa-level.** Sect massacres, torture, assassination, betrayal, crippling, blood feuds, war atrocities — described plainly.
4. **Reader comments removed** in murim. Pure novel-style text.
5. **Birth fully random** (BitLife).
6. **Add top-tier figures** (see 8.7).

### NPC feelings (applies to everyone)
Every NPC has: personality (2–3 traits), mood, **memory of what you did to them** (gifts, insults, help, betrayal), affinity, ambitions.
They act on their own each year: marry, have kids, rise/fall in rank, take revenge, ask you for help, gossip about you,
get jealous (wives, rival disciples), grieve when someone dies. Relationships can turn — a friend can become a rival, a rival a sworn brother.

### 8.7 Emperor, Heavenly Demon, Demon Lord, Immortals
- **ฮ่องเต้ (Emperor)** — the court above the jianghu. Imperial edicts can ban sects, draft martial artists for war, or hire them.
  Paths: imperial guard / Embroidered Uniform Guard (secret police), eunuch faction, palace physician, general, minister,
  marry into the imperial clan, assassination plots, rebellion → in chaos era you can **become Emperor** (found a dynasty).
  Each Emperor is an NPC with personality (wise / tyrant / puppet / paranoid) and changes every reign.
- **มารสวรรค์ (Heavenly Demon / 천마)** — leader of the Demonic Cult. One per generation, chosen by brutal succession fights.
  You can join the cult, rise to **Heavenly Demon** yourself, or be the hero who stops their invasion of the Central Plains.
- **จอมมาร (Demon Lord)** — separate from the Heavenly Demon: a calamity-level evil (blood arts that eat lives, a sealed ancient
  demon, or a mad genius). Appears rarely; triggers a "world crisis" arc where all factions must unite. Player can become one (dark path).
- **เทพเซียน (Immortals)** — the hidden tier above all murim realms. After 생사경 the rare few can attempt
  **ascension/immortal tribulation** (already partly in code). Immortals rarely appear: guide a chosen one, punish heaven-defying acts,
  leave relics. Late-game goal: become an immortal and leave the mortal world — counts as a "true ending" of a bloodline.

## 14. Choices: pros, cons and success %

Every choice card shows what you are risking. Example (escort ambushed by bandits):

```
⚔️ ชักกระบี่สู้                      สำเร็จ 62%
   ✅ ชื่อเสียง +, ค่าจ้างเต็ม, ศิษย์สำนักคุ้มภัยนับถือ
   ❌ ล้มเหลว: บาดเจ็บหนัก (อาจเสียอวัยวะ), สินค้าถูกปล้น
   ⚠️ อัตรารอด 91%
🗣️ เจรจาจ่ายค่าผ่านทาง               สำเร็จ 78%  (สติปัญญา + ชื่อเสียง)
   ✅ ไม่มีใครเจ็บ   ❌ เสียเงิน 30 ตำลึง, ถูกมองว่าขี้ขลาด
🏃 ทิ้งสินค้าหนี                      สำเร็จ 95%
   ✅ รอดแน่   ❌ สำนักคุ้มภัยไล่ออก, หนี้ชดใช้สินค้า
🗡️ ซัดอาวุธลับใส่หัวหน้าโจร (ถ้ามีวิชา) สำเร็จ 44%
```

Rules:
- % comes from the character: relevant stat(s) + realm + technique mastery + injuries + equipment + NPC opponent strength. Shown exactly.
- Each choice lists **pros (✅)** and **cons (❌)** in one line each; hidden outcomes show as "❓ ไม่แน่ชัด" (mystery is part of the fun).
- Outcomes have 4 tiers: **สำเร็จงดงาม / สำเร็จ / พลาด / พลาดหนัก**, not just win/lose.
- Survival warning appears whenever death is possible.
- Some choices only appear with a requirement (technique, item, relationship, alignment) and say why: "(ใช้วิชาสายฟ้าฟาดได้)".

## 15. Body & injuries (real and permanent)

The body is tracked part by part:

| Part | Possible damage | Lasting effect |
|---|---|---|
| Eyes (L/R) | scratched, **blinded** | one eye: accuracy −, looks −; both: cannot read manuals, must learn "listening to the wind" style |
| Ears | deaf | ambush checks −, sound-based techniques fail |
| Tongue | cut out (torture) | cannot persuade/teach by talking, writes instead |
| Face | scar, burn | looks −; can make you feared (intimidation +) |
| Arms (L/R), hands, fingers | broken, tendons cut, **fingers lost**, **arm severed** | weapon techniques of that hand −70% until you retrain the other hand (years); "one-armed swordsman" path |
| Legs (L/R) | broken, tendons cut, **leg severed** | qinggong lost, escape −, wooden leg from a craftsman restores walking only |
| Torso / organs | internal injury (light → critical), broken ribs | health cap −, yearly death risk until healed |
| Meridians / **dantian** | blocked, damaged, **shattered** | internal energy drops or goes to 0; rare cures only (legendary medicine / reverse meridians) |
| Whole body | poisoned, infection, chronic pain | yearly health loss, happiness − |

- Injuries heal over **years**, not instantly. Age slows healing. Treatment cost: village healer < physician < divine physician.
- **Severed parts do not grow back.** Only a divine physician within days, or a legendary relic, can reattach — very rare.
- Prosthetics: wooden leg, iron hand (can become a hidden weapon!), eye patch.
- Disabilities show next to the name (e.g. 🦾 แขนขวาขาด, 👁️ ตาซ้ายบอด) and change the available choices and %.
- Torture events (captured by a cult, interrogated by imperial guards) can cost fingers, eyes, tongue.
- Death is real: bleed-out, poison, executions, plague, old age.

## 16. Combat flow

Big fights are short scenes (1–3 rounds), small fights resolve in one choice.
Each round you pick: **โจมตีเต็มกำลัง · ตั้งรับ · ใช้ท่าไม้ตาย (costs energy) · อาวุธลับ/ยาพิษ · หลบหนี · ยอมแพ้/ขอชีวิต**, each with %.
Result per round: damage to a random/targeted body part (see §15), energy spent, opponent state.
Opponent NPCs remember: a spared enemy may repay the favor — or return stronger for revenge.

Target: **600+ murim events** at launch of the overhaul. Each has 2–4 choices with % and pros/cons, and most leave a lasting mark
(flag, NPC memory, injury, item, technique, reputation).

- Young-master humiliation at a banquet → slap / duel / patience.

- Found your sect / family; disciples' rivalry; spy inside your sect.

### Elder (51+) — ~70 events
- Closed-door retreat for a breakthrough (deadly).
- Choosing a successor; disciples split the sect.
- Writing your manual; burying it or passing it on.

### World-tier arcs (any age, era-driven) — ~50 events
- **Heavenly Demon succession war**, Demonic Cult crusade on the Central Plains.

- **Emperor** changes: wise / tyrant / puppet; palace coup; dynasty collapse → warlord era → found a dynasty.

### Generational callbacks — woven into all of the above
- Meet descendants of people you helped or wronged; your old sect asks for help; your grave; your manual found by a stranger;

### 18.1 Life goals (new core — ties everything together)
- Player and every important NPC: **1 main goal, up to 2 side goals, a fear, values, limits**.
- Goal types: revenge · protect family · found/restore a sect · peak realm · wealth & freedom · rise at court · famous craftsman/physician/merchant ·
  truth of my birth · keep an oath · atone/quit violence · pass on knowledge · quiet family life · become Emperor / Heavenly Demon / sect leader · ascend.
- Each goal = start condition → visible steps → risks/costs → several endings → writes to memory, fame, relations, Chronicle.
- Goals **change for a reason** (mother dies → healer instead of warrior; revenge turns to forgiveness when the truth surfaces).
- Success is possible without being a top fighter.

### 18.2 Year structure (replaces plain "tap +1 year")
1. Status summary (age, health, money/debt, injuries, open threads).
2. **Main plan for the year**: train · work · travel · run sect · family · investigate (one pick; "repeat last plan" button).
3. Side activities (limited by time/money).
4. World ticks (NPCs, economy, sects, court).
5. 0–2 events from real conditions. Pacing: calm / pressure / turning point / past catches up / rare chance — not drama every year.
6. Year result card (income, training, relations, injuries), short and expandable.
7. Chronicle entry if important.
8. Age up, stage change.
- **Skip 3 years / closed-door 1–3 years**: warns of known risks first and **stops early on important events**.

### 18.3 NPCs: memory, knowledge, news
- Important NPCs (~50–150 per save) are detailed; everyone else is a light profile or an aggregate (phone performance).
- **Weighted memories**: major (saved a life, killed a lover, betrayal, oath, took as disciple, destroyed sect) / medium (loan, duel, favor) / minor.
  Each stores who, to whom, year, place, what they actually know, their interpretation, emotion, how public.
- **No omniscient NPCs**: they learn by witnessing, reports, rumors. News travels slowly between regions and can be distorted.
  Disguise, forged papers and destroying evidence lower exposure.
- NPC decisions = goal + personality + relations + resources + risk + what they know (+ small randomness).
- Accept: no revenge for things they could not know; the dead stay dead.

### 18.4 Relationships are multi-axis (replaces single affinity)
Axes: **love · trust · respect · fear · debt (บุญคุณ) · jealousy · grudge · loyalty**. The UI shows only those that matter.
Someone can love you but not trust you; an expensive gift does not erase a betrayal.
Actions: talk, gift by taste, help, train together, keep a promise, apologize/compensate, reveal a secret, ask for help, end the relation.
- Children: own personality, talents, goals; may refuse the heir role or leave; favoritism breeds conflict.
- Multiple spouses: each has consent, goals, relations with the other spouses, her family's expectations, children's care — never a collection bonus.
- Disciples grow into people with their own goals.

### 18.5 Techniques give playstyle; training modes differ
- Each technique: type, range, usage style, body/qi requirement, strengths/weaknesses, compatibility, training cost, misuse risk, owner, teaching rules.
- Mastery unlocks **options**, not only numbers (basic moves → fewer mistakes → applications → strategy → beyond form).
- Training modes: safe/slow · hard/fast/risky · with a master (costs, precise) · from a manual (can misread) · real fights (exp, risk) · closed-door (big gain, world moves on).
- Creating a technique needs comprehension + experience + base arts + time + resources; it starts unstable and improves with use.

### 18.6 Honest odds (merges §14 with expansion §7.1)
- **Exact %** only when the character can judge (own skills, known opponent). **Unknown opponents/situations show ต่ำ/กลาง/สูง or a range**;
  observing, spying, earlier fights or reputation narrow it to an exact %.
- The % shown is the formula used — never changed after the click. Balance tests check that 60% choices win ≈60% over many runs.
- Death-risk warnings based on what the character could know.

### 18.7 Injuries open new paths (merges §15)
Severe injuries stay permanent (user decision), but each leaves a **meaningful way to keep playing**: one-armed → retrain the other hand or teach;
dantian lost → external arts, medicine, trade, investigation; blind → listening arts; lame → prosthetic or new role; chronic poison → manage money & medicine.
Violence has consequences: fear, resistance, survivors' memories, law, feuds across generations; it is not always the best option.
**Opponents adapt**: survivors study your style, prepare antidotes, gather allies, flee, surrender or reconcile — from information, never hidden stat boosts.

### 18.8 Sects, economy, court, eras
- **Sect profile**: leader & successor, goals, doctrine, money, manpower, territory, signature arts, weaknesses, internal problems, allies/enemies, legitimacy.
  Faction relations by trust/interest/territory/debts/ideology/fear/betrayal history. Orthodox not all good, unorthodox not all evil, the Cult has its own ideology and infighting.
- **Sect management**: budget, recruitment, loyalty, morale, buildings with real effects, side businesses, defense, alliances, succession, spies.
- **Economy**: seasons, harvest, drought, flood, plague, war, trade routes, bandits, taxes → cost of living, medicine prices, wages, debts, escort risk.
  Defined money sources and sinks, no infinite money loops, capped inflation; tested for both rich and poor lives.
- **Imperial Court & Inner Palace** (expansion §8.5, adopted in full): Emperor limited by legitimacy, health, army, treasury, popularity;
  Empress with her own goals and clan; consorts with backgrounds and alliances (not all enemies); eunuchs whose power is access, documents, news and patronage
  (not all villains); crown prince & royals; ministers, generals and great clans who switch sides by interest.
  Succession by legal status + ability + backers + legitimacy + secrets; no clear heir → other branch, regency, compromise, or succession war.
  Player paths: guard/investigator, palace physician, supplier/lender, official, messenger/spy, heir of a fallen clan. Access must be earned.
  Example chains: lost edict · suspected crown prince · contested appointment · eunuch with a secret · palace grain crisis · regency · clearing an ancestor's name.
  Not every save gets a coup; the player can stay out of politics and still feel the effects later.
- **Rumors & investigation** (expansion §10, §8.5.5): five layers — fact · witnesses/evidence · rumor · each character's belief · what the player knows.
  Rumors have a source, time, reliability, motive, a way to verify and trap risk. Frames and forgeries leave traces; not every case is solvable.
- **Eras by pressure**, not a timer: legitimacy, food security, public anger, warlord power, sect conflict, plague, trade.
  Crises run 5 stages: warning → preparation/negotiation → crisis → outcome → long tail (refugees, war debt, revenge, rebuilding).
- Legendary figures stay rare, and the story explains why they do not solve everything.

### 18.9 Generations: what passes and what does not
- Passes: part of money/property, family weapon, house, business, sect, manuals, taught techniques, reputation, debts, oaths, allies, enemies, rumors, secrets, unfinished goals.
- Does **not** pass automatically: full mastery, every personal relationship, disciples' loyalty, positions that need recognition, truths the new generation cannot know, power that needs upkeep.
- Before switching: summary of successes, failures, people affected, open promises, inheritable assets, family/sect state.
- On death, when fitting: pass on arts, write a will, settle inheritance, reconcile, reveal a secret — never forced on sudden deaths.

### 18.11 Conflicts between the two documents and how they are resolved
1. **"Rebuild all events from scratch" vs "don't mass-delete, audit first"** → rewrite all murim content, but delete old files only after new content
   covers that life stage and tests pass; engine systems that work are reused.
2. **"Always show exact %" vs "no false precision"** → exact % when knowable, ต่ำ/กลาง/สูง or a range when not (§18.6).
3. **"Manhwa-level dark" vs "not every event dramatic; no violence as cheap thrill"** → dark content stays (massacres, torture, severed limbs)
   but always with context and consequences; calm years in between; violence against women or eunuchs is never used just for shock.
4. **"Permanent injuries" vs "injury must not end the game"** → permanent, each with a playable alternative path.
5. **"Every NPC has real feelings" vs phone performance** → full simulation for important NPCs only; the rest are light/aggregate.
6. **BitLife one-tap year vs structured year** → keep a fast path: "repeat last plan + age up" in one tap; full planning optional.
7. **Saves**: current murim saves cannot move cleanly into the new systems → new murim lives start fresh after Phase 1;
   an old save stays playable in legacy mode until that life ends (save-version check).
