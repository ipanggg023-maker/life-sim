# Release 21 draft names: ancient dragons (approved 2026-11-06 after a rename to ancient epic names)

Five dragons older than history, all alive before year 1. They are new and separate from the world's current dragons (วาลเกรียส, เนริธ, คอร์วาธ, ซิลแฟ, อัมบรา, มอร์ดราค). Each is linked to two of the weapons from Release 20.

| Dragon | Element | Where it sleeps | Linked weapons and why |
|---|---|---|---|
| อาซาร์โกธ ผู้ฝันโลกให้เกิด | The first, older than all (light and dark) | ห้องหัวใจใต้เทือกเขาบรรพกาล | Fits the existing legend that the world grew from a sleeping first dragon and the mountains are its scales. Excalibur (King Aldric's oath was sworn on its scale) and the time staff (the future it shows is this dragon's dream). |
| ไวร์มัคซาร์ เพลิงแรกแห่งกาลก่อนกาล | Fire | ทะเลลาวาใต้ภูเขาไฟสามยอด (Varn) | The dragon-slaying spear (Gorm forged it to kill this one and was burned alive; "it isn't dead, only sleeping") and the eternal ember staff (Ignis drew his fire from its breath). |
| เลวีอาธัล มารดาแห่งห้วงลึกทั้งเจ็ด | Water | ร่องทะเลลึกนอกอ่าวเกลเวน | The dragon horn (Captain Rina's fleet and dragon were swallowed when it turned in its sleep) and the thunder king's crown (the old council's hall sank in the same tide). |
| โกลโมรอธ ผู้แบกภูผาบนหลัง | Earth | ใจกลางเทือกเขาเหล็ก | The mountain-splitting axe (the mountain that fell on Torga was its back stirring) and the star-forging hammer (Durin's first falling star landed on its back). |
| เซราฟาเอล ผู้ไม่เคยแตะพื้นโลก | Wind | ตาพายุนิรันดร์เหนือทะเลเหนือ | The crescent moon bow (Lena's last arrow was shot at its shadow across the moon) and the eternal bulwark (Sir Gareth's gate held against its storm for three days). |

What you can do with each, as the spec says: find it (rumours, then its lair), wake it, fight it, bargain with it, or learn from it. The world remembers the outcome for later lives: a slain one leaves a lair you can visit, a befriended one remembers your family name, and one woken and left raging burns towns or starts a world crisis.
