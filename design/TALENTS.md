# Talents rework (grill session, October 2026)

Murim only. Today's talents are mostly passive number bonuses; the goal is talents that shape the life you play.

## Decisions

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| What makes them fun | All four: talent storylines, awakening and evolution, double-edged talents, combos |
| Source | Random at birth only (no earning, inheriting or losing talents) |
| Curses | Yes, each with a hidden upside that can turn it around |
| Count | 1–3 per character, each one big |
| Visibility | Common talents shown at birth; rare and above hidden until an event reveals them, and the reveal is a story beat |
| Evolution | Through deeds, through crisis, and at a fork where you pick a light or dark version |
| Price of double-edged talents | Heavy but survivable: real costs a careful player can manage or cure |
| Birth roll | Pure random (as now) |
| Catalogue | About 30: rework the existing 20 and add about 10, each with an arc, an evolution and a price |
| Themes to add | Special physiques, mind and insight, fate and stars, crafts and demonic arts |
| World reaction | Sects compete for you, you can be hunted for your talent, throne and prophecy react, plus personal arcs |
| Combos | Hidden; a combo triggers with a special scene the first time, then it's listed in the Soul tab |

## Shape of one talent

Each talent has:
- **Effect**: what it changes in play, not only numbers.
- **Reveal**: the scene that uncovers it, for talents hidden at birth.
- **Arc**: two to four events that only this talent opens, spread across life stages.
- **Evolution**:
  - a deed trigger (do the talent's thing enough), a crisis trigger (near death, loss, failed breakthrough), or both;
  - leads to a fork with a light and a dark version.
- **Price**: for double-edged talents, the cost and how it can be managed or cured.
- **Curse upside**: for curses, the hidden way to turn it around.
- **World reaction**: who wants you, who hunts you, who fears you.
- **Combos**: other talents it resonates with, and what the first combo scene does.
