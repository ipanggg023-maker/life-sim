# Fantasy talent stars: body and magic, 0–10

These decisions came out of a design interview with the user on 2026-11-04.

## Scale and birth roll
- Two star counts, body and magic, each 0–10. Magic moves from 0–5 to 0–10; old saves convert old×2.
- Steep pyramid: about 30% roll 0★, most land at 1–3, 7★ ≈ 1/50, 9★ ≈ 1/500, 10★ ≈ 1/2000.
- Race shifts the roll gently. Orc, beastfolk and dwarf get +1 body. Elf and demon get +1 magic. Dragon gets +1 to both. Human and halfling stay neutral but roll wider.

## What body stars do (all four)
- **Head start:** starting body points, health and looks. A 9–10★ child starts at rank 1–2.
- **Rank ceiling:** this works like magicCap. Low stars stall early unless a relic, saga or evolution lifts the cap.
- **Training speed:** stars add to training gains and to breakthrough odds.
- **Star arts:** body arts unlock at set star counts. Examples: 7★ iron skin, 9★ a rock-splitting strike, 10★ slow aging.

## Rank ladder
Each track grows to 7 ranks by adding 2 mythic ones:
- Body: Sword Saint, then Sword God.
- Magic: Sage, then Mage of the Stars.

The mythic ranks need about 8★ and 10★, or a rare cap-lifter.

## Changing stars
- Stars are fixed at birth. Rare boosts give +1: talent evolution, sagas, demon pacts, some relics.
- Heirs inherit near the parent's stars, ±1. A child rolls from both parents' stars.

## Reveal
- At birth the stars show as ??? with hints, such as a strong baby or a candle that bends.
- At age 6 a forced awakening scene at the temple reads them. A 10★ reading shakes the town.

## Zero stars
- **Late bloomer:** about a 5% chance that a sleeping star wakes between ages 20 and 40, usually after a crisis.
- **Other paths stay strong:** careers, trade, the throne and family don't need stars.
- **Underdog legend:** beating someone 5★ or more above you gives big fame and a deed.

## People
Key people have stars shown on their person card: rivals, spouse, children, masters and great enemies.

## A high-star life (all four)
- **Recruiters and patrons** from 7★.
- **Envy and danger** from 8★: rivals, assassins, demons.
- **Weight of expectation:** slacking costs joy and standing. A 10★ who quits becomes a known disgrace.
- **Legend status:** a 10★ is written into the world chronicle at birth, and later lives hear of them.

## Dual gift
With 6★ or more in both tracks:
- The Spellblade path opens. The weaker track counts for 60% in combat instead of 30%.
- Spellblade arts become available, along with the rare title "Twin Star".

## Talents
- Magic talents are rescaled: +1 becomes +2, Archmage seed sets 9, The Chosen sets 9★/9★.
- Each magic talent gets a body twin at every rarity, for example Iron-boned +2 and Titan's seed sets 9.
- Exactly 10★ stays mostly a birth-roll event.

## Balance target
A 10★ is a prodigy, not invincible:
- As a teen, they beat grown knights.
- They reach Master by about 25.
- Dragons, demon lords and bad luck can still kill them.
- The mythic ranks take most of a life.

## Display (all four)
- **Power tab header:** two rows of 10 stars, plus a tier name such as Talented, Prodigy or Once in an age.
- **Life summary and chronicle.**
- **Person cards.**
- **New achievements:** born 10★, 0★ to Master, Twin Star, underdog victory.
