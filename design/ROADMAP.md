# Murim roadmap — from the 2026-10-22 grill session

Decisions the player made (these override older docs where they differ):

- **Biggest gap:** the jianghu world doesn't feel alive yet.
- **Difficulty:** keep it as it is (careful median age ~81, random ~51, peak realm ~10% of cultivators).
- **Other worlds:** stay locked — jianghu only until it is complete.
- **Dark actions:** the player may poison or assassinate **anyone**, family included. Odds depend on how well
  protected the target is (an emperor is nearly impossible). Consequences are heavy and permanent.
- **Failing against someone high-ranked** (official, sect master, palace, emperor): execution **and a purge of the
  family** — some descendants escape and carry the revenge into the next generation.
- **Romance drama wanted:** forbidden love with enemies/rival sects; affairs, third parties, jealousy (both ways);
  arranged and political marriages; same-sex love (with the social pressure of the era).
- **Every NPC's gender must be obvious.**
- **Events must really happen:** if a scene says someone died, they are dead.

Earlier backlog, now done:
- Three Kingdoms courts: each kingdom has a capital, an ageing lord and heir, a chief strategist and general, and war news. Lords die and heirs succeed (or an uncle seizes the throne), weak kingdoms fall, and your lord's trust brings rewards or suspicion. A general can seize the capital and, by unifying the land, become emperor.
- Chronic-poison deaths: the cause was a label, not the mechanics. Natural deaths picked "accumulated poison" or "qi burst apart" at random. Causes now come from the body (real poison, internal wounds, a shattered dantian), the region's illnesses, age and realm. The divine healer can now purge permanent poison.
- Era events: 33 events, plus 8 kingdom-court events, that make peace, decline, chaos, the Three Kingdoms and recovery feel different to live through.

Emperor depth (player request, October 2026):
- Taking the throne has many routes: declare yourself, force the puppet to abdicate, or march troops into the palace. The steps on the way up include setting the princes against each other, buying the gate eunuch, marrying your child into the palace, spreading omens, slow poison, and selling out co-conspirators.
- Your full name is the imperial title. Your family gets imperial titles:
  - mother: ไทเฮา (Empress Dowager)
  - father: ไท่ซ่างหวง (Retired Emperor)
  - siblings: อ๋อง (prince-king) or องค์หญิงใหญ่ (Grand Princess)
  - children: องค์ชาย (prince) or องค์หญิง (princess)
  - spouse: ฮองเฮา (Empress) or พระราชสวามี (Emperor's Consort)
- Full harem. A female emperor has male consorts; same-sex consorts are possible.
  - Selection, nights, rank ladder, cold palace.
  - Favourite and jealousy; consort clans; poisoned children; fake pregnancy; secret lovers; neglected consorts.
  - Mothers lobbying for their sons; princes competing.
- Ruling:
  - Daily audience, imperial tour, heaven rites, palace exam.
  - Edicts, nomad tribute, final appeals.
  - Naming or adopting an heir, and abdication.
- One empire with a prince-king (อ๋อง) for each region. Kings age, die, rebel, recruit you, tax you and summon you; as emperor you must control them.
- In the Three Kingdoms era each kingdom holds its regions and its lord is emperor. A fallen kingdom's regions are annexed for good, and a new dynasty appoints new kings.

Edicts and rankings (player request, October 2026):
- Edicts stay in force, take effect every year until repealed or expired, can't contradict each other, and are capped at six at once.
  - 22 edicts in four groups: economy, society, Jianghu, state. Ministers explain the trade-offs before you choose.
  - NPC emperors issue edicts that suit their temperament.
  - Edicts reach ordinary lives: martial registration, conscription, the martial exam, raids on gambling dens, land reform, cult purges, sects ordered shut, summons to court, corvée labour, salt prices, heavy tax and tax cuts, census, granaries, caravans, inspectors, resettlement.
  - The world reacts to your edicts: sects rising, great clans petitioning, eunuch plots, peasants' gratitude, prince-kings refusing.
- Rankings:
  - The main list has 20 places; the top ten are the ten great masters, and places 11–20 are reachable mid-career. When a top master dies, the successor is about as strong.
  - The youth list is now ทำเนียบมังกร (20 places, men and women). You keep a place until you're beaten.
  - Challenge activities let you pick someone one to three places above you and fight a real duel. The main list offers a sparring match or a fight to the death.
  - People below you challenge you back, and the ทำเนียบมังกร tournament is held every three years.
