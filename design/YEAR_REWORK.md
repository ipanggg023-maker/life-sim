# Fantasy year rework: every +1 year is a chapter

These decisions come from a design interview with the user on 2026-11-04. The rework covers the fantasy world only.

## The problem
The user finds the events after +1 year boring for four reasons:
- The text is flat and too short.
- The choices are obvious.
- The results don't matter.
- The events are random and disconnected from your life.

## The shape of a year
- **One headline per year.** The headline is a multi-beat scene in 2–4 beats: a setup, a twist that depends on your first choice, a final choice, then a payoff. Each beat is a short, vivid paragraph.
- **Small moments.** Each year also has 2–4 small moments with no popup. They are resolved automatically by your personality ("you do what you'd do"), and their effects still apply.
- **Chapter card.** Each year opens as a chapter with:
  - a generated title, for example "อายุ 23 · ฤดูหนาวแห่งหนี้ก้อนโต"
  - 2–4 sentences of narration that weave the small moments together
  - then the headline scene
- **Forced moments keep their own popup:** the star reading, breakthroughs, the end of a war and similar.

## Content
- The ~600 existing short events become small moments, chosen by personality.
- About 80 new multi-beat headline scenes are written. They are spread across childhood, youth, adulthood and old age, every race, job group, family situation and world state.
- Echoes, people drama, work, court and war events all compete for the headline slot. The losers become small moments.

## Choosing the headline
A headline is drawn from your life. Candidates score by how many hooks they have into it, for example:
- your spouse, a child, your boss or an enemy
- a seed you planted
- your job or your rank
- the current omen or war

The most tied candidate wins.

## Dilemmas
- Every choice costs something; there is no obviously right answer.
- A short line under each choice hints at what it risks.

## Every headline leaves all four
- a memory in someone
- a seed or a rumor
- a change in you: personality, a vow, a scar or a title
- a change in your world: money, home, job, standing or the kingdom

## QoL
- **Auto-decide toggle:** off by default. When on, your personality also decides the headlines.
- **Instant text:** the setting is more visible and remembered.
- **Recap chips:** under the chapter, chips show what changed this year (money, health, people, standing).
- **Re-read last chapter:** a button reopens last year's chapter card.

## Balance
Effects per year must stay close to the old totals. Check this with the bot: wealth, health, deaths and ranks should be comparable to before.
